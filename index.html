Hari Govind

President & CEO, brk tech — a Berkshire Hathaway company

Hari Govind leads brk tech, a new kind of technology organization built to transform Berkshire Hathaway’s businesses by empowering them with a cutting-edge tech & AI advantage — turning technology buyers into builders.

About

Hari is a trusted technology advisor to CEOs and CxOs across the Berkshire portfolio, bringing a rare combination of Silicon Valley innovation and enterprise-scale execution to brk tech.

“I’m passionate about building purpose driven teams who deliver great products. I care deeply about engineering excellence and fostering a creative ecosystem where people can innovate at the speed of trust to deliver compelling experiences.”

At BNSF, he led the railroad’s transformation into an AI-driven organization as CTO, launching bnsf | tech and hiring 100 engineers from state colleges across Wisconsin, Illinois, Michigan, Texas, Oklahoma, and Florida. At GEICO, he spearheaded one of the most ambitious technology overhauls in the insurance industry.

Prior to Berkshire, Hari held senior leadership roles at Facebook, Microsoft, and Honeywell over two decades — giving him deep fluency across consumer platforms, enterprise systems, and industrial operations. He now leads brk tech at the inflection of technology, artificial intelligence, open source innovation, and a builders mindset.
